Reporting and blocking

How to report

Every profile, review, feed post, album comment, song comment and Community Choice pick has a Report button, and every profile has a Block button that hides that person from your feeds. The person you report or block is never told who did it.

Reports go to a moderation queue that a person reviews. We aim to look at every report within 48 hours, and sooner for anything involving a child’s safety, threats or illegal content. When a review is finished you get a notification saying whether we took action.

If we remove something you posted, you are told what was removed and why. Serious or repeated breaches can lead to your account being suspended. Words that are never allowed (slurs and targeted abuse) are refused automatically when you post.

Complaints and appeals. If you disagree with a decision, or want to complain about how we handled a report, email vinall.support@gmail.com. A different decision is made by a person, and we reply within 14 days.

If you see something that puts someone in immediate danger, contact the emergency services (999 in the UK) first.

Keeping under-18s safe

VINALL is for people aged 13 and over, and some of the people using it are under 18. This is what is in place to protect them, and what to do if something goes wrong.

  • Private by default. If you are 13–17, your ratings and notes start visible only to friends (people you follow who follow you back). That keeps them out of the public feed, album reviews, Taste Match and people suggestions. Your username, profile photo, bio, comments, lists, follows and Groove memberships can still be seen by others, so choose a username and photo that do not identify you.
  • No private messages. VINALL has no direct messages or group chat, and it never uses your location.
  • Keep your details to yourself. Never share your address, school, phone number or other accounts, and never agree to meet someone you met on VINALL. If anyone asks, report them with Asking to meet up or for personal details.
  • Bullying. Block anyone who is unkind to you: they disappear from your feeds, comments, notifications and search, and they are not told. Slurs and targeted abuse are refused automatically. Report anything else.
  • Harmful content. Content that encourages suicide, self-harm or eating disorders, dangerous challenges, or drug use is not allowed and is removed when reported.
  • Safety reports come first. Reports about a child’s safety, someone asking to meet or for personal details, threats, self-harm or illegal content go to the top of the moderation queue, and we aim to review them within 24 hours. Where a child may be at risk, we may pass information to the police or the National Crime Agency.
If you need to talk to someone

If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999. Childline (under 19): 0800 1111 or childline.org.uk, free and 24/7. Samaritans: 116 123, free and 24/7. You can also report worries about online sexual abuse to CEOP at ceop.police.uk.

Parents or carers who are worried about an account, or who believe a child under 13 is using VINALL, can email vinall.support@gmail.com.
