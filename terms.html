What you post

Your ratings, notes and lore stay yours. By posting them you allow VINALL to store and display them in the app, including to other users where you have made them public.

Do not post anything that is:

  • abusive, harassing, hateful or threatening

Reporting. Every profile has a Report button, and a Block button that hides that person from your feeds. Reports are reviewed and accounts that break these rules can be removed without warning.

VINALL is provided as-is, with no guarantee that it will be available, or that nothing will ever be lost. Keep your own copy of anything you would be upset to lose. It is a hobby project, not a bank.

Ending things

You can delete your account at any time from your profile, which removes everything immediately. We can suspend or remove accounts that break these terms.
