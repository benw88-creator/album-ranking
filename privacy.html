What is stored

Your accountThe email address you sign up with, and an encrypted password. Handled by Supabase Auth; we never see your password. If you sign in with Apple or Google instead, we receive the email address (or Apple’s private relay address) and the name they share, and no password.
Your musicThe albums and songs you rate, your scores, notes, lists and diary entries.
Your loreThe answers you tap on Today, plus any optional note you add.
Your profileUsername, avatar, bio, and anything else you choose to fill in.
Imported listening historyOptional. If you upload your own Spotify data, VINALL stores how many times you played each track and for how long, both in total and per day, so Replay can show what you played in a given week, month or year. Individual plays are added up in your browser and never uploaded; only those totals are stored. Nobody else can see them, and you can remove them from your profile without affecting anything else.
Imported libraryIf you import your Spotify library, the songs, albums and playlists you choose become ratings, lists or a shortlist in VINALL. To match them to the catalogue, each track's title, artist and Spotify link are sent to VINALL's server; matches are cached without anything that identifies you.
Usage eventsWhich questions were shown, answered or skipped, so we can tell whether the app is any good. These are tied to your account but contain no message content.
Crash reportsIf something breaks, the error message and page address are recorded so it can be fixed.
NotificationsOnly if you switch them on. On the website, turning on notifications stores the address your browser gives us for delivering them; the message is encrypted and passed through your browser’s own push service (Apple, Google, Microsoft or Mozilla, depending on the browser), which cannot read it. Turning them off, or logging out, deletes that address. In the app, reminders are scheduled on your phone itself and nothing is stored with us.
Blocks and reportsIf you block someone, we store the fact that you blocked them. If you report something, we store what you reported, why, and who you are, so it can be reviewed. The person you block or report is never told who did it.

Stored on your device

VINALL keeps a working copy of your ratings, diary entries, game progress and Replay summaries in your browser’s storage, so the app is fast and works when the connection drops. It also stores your login session and the theme you have equipped. None of this is a tracking cookie and none of it is shared with anyone. Clearing your browser data for this site removes all of it; anything already synced to your account comes back when you sign in.

What other people can see

VINALL is partly social, so some of this is public by design.

  • Public — your username, avatar, bio, ratings (unless you choose Friends or Just me), lists, lore answers you have not marked private, comments, Community Choice picks, who you follow and who follows you, Groove memberships, and game leaderboard scores.
  • Private — your email address, your usage events, any listening history you imported, and anything you mark private.

Your ratings visibility can be changed at any time on your profile. Just me and Friends (people you follow who follow you back) also keep your scores and notes out of the public Crate feed and album reviews, and switching away from Anyone removes what you had already posted there.

Other services

SupabaseStores the database and handles logins. EU region (eu-west-1).
VercelHosts the site.
DeezerProvides album artwork, track listings, catalogue information and the 30-second preview clips VINALL plays. When you search, or open an album page, the words you searched for and the album and artist names are sent to Deezer so it can answer. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them, and browsing VINALL does not tell Deezer who you are.
YouTubePlays Community Choice moments, through YouTube’s own embedded player (youtube.com). Nothing loads from YouTube until you agree to it, which we ask the first time a pick would play from YouTube (you can say no and keep the Deezer preview); once a video plays, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to that player. When you pick a moment, the song and artist name are sent to YouTube to find the video, with nothing that identifies you.
AppleProvides a small number of preview clips and cover images where Deezer has none. The same applies: album and artist names only, nothing that identifies you.
SpotifyUsed for one thing only — looking up an artist so streaming totals can be found for the Bid Wars and Collection valuations. No personal data is sent, and VINALL no longer uses Spotify for artwork or catalogue information.
DiscogsUsed only when you start a Bid War in Score mode, to look up how the Discogs community rates a record. The album and artist names are sent so it can answer; nothing that identifies you is.
kworb.netProvides public streaming totals used by the Bid Wars game. No personal data is sent there.
MusicBrainzUsed by Music Passport to find out which country an artist is from. Your browser asks MusicBrainz directly, so it sees your IP address and the artist names, and nothing else about you.
jsDelivrDelivers the code library VINALL uses to talk to its database. Your browser downloads it from jsDelivr’s network, which sees your IP address as part of that request.
Apple and Google sign-inOnly if you choose to sign in with them. They confirm who you are and share your email address and name with VINALL under their own privacy policies.

Deleting everything

Immediate and permanent

Go to your profile and choose Delete my account. This removes your ratings, lore, lists, grooves, profile, avatar, any listening history you imported, and your login. It cannot be undone, and there is no waiting period.

Age

You must be 13 or over to use VINALL. When you first sign in we ask which group you are in (under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over) and keep only that answer, never your date of birth. Accounts for under-13s are signed out and closed.

If you are 13–17, your ratings start visible to friends only, which also keeps them out of the public feed. You can change that, but please do not post anything that identifies where you live or go to school. VINALL has no direct messages, does not use your location, and does not show advertising. If you believe someone under 13 has an account, contact us and it will be removed.

How long it is kept

Your account and your musicFor as long as you have an account. Deleting your account removes them straight away.
Usage events and crash reportsUsage events are deleted after 13 months, long enough to compare the same season a year apart. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days, by which time any bug they point to has been fixed or superseded. Both stop being linked to you as soon as your account is deleted.
ReportsReports about an account or post are kept after a review is finished, with the decision, because a record of what was decided is the only way to be consistent about it. Reports you filed yourself are deleted with your account.
SuspensionsA suspended account can no longer sign in. Its data is kept until it is deleted, so that an appeal can be reviewed.

Why we are allowed to store it

If you are in the UK or EU, the legal bases are contract for your account and the music you rate, since the app cannot work without them, and legitimate interests for usage events, crash reports and moderation records, which keep the app working and safe.

Features such as Replay, Music DNA, About You and Taste Match analyse your own ratings and listening to describe your taste. That analysis is shown to you (and, for Taste Match, to the person you compare with, only where your ratings are public). It is not used for advertising and no decision with legal or similarly significant effect is made from it.

Where it is held

The database and logins are in Supabase’s EU region (eu-west-1). Hosting is Vercel, whose network is global, so a page request may be served from outside the UK or EU. Both are bound by their own data-processing terms.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be erased, ask us to restrict how it is used, or object to the uses based on legitimate interests. You can download your ratings yourself at any time (Settings, Your ratings, Export). Deleting your account erases it immediately. For anything else, email us and you will get an answer within one month.

Complaints about your data

If you think we have handled your personal data wrongly, you can complain to us directly. Email vinall.support@gmail.com with “Data protection complaint” in the subject (any message that is clearly a complaint counts, however it is worded). We will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it properly, keep you updated if it takes longer, and tell you the outcome and what we have done about it.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. They usually expect you to have raised it with us first.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is stored or who can see it, the effective date at the top of this page changes with it and the app will say so. Minor corrections and clarifications are made without notice.
