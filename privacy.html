Privacy

Last updated 8 September 2026

VINALL is a small independent site for rating music and keeping track of what your records mean to you. This page says plainly what it stores and who can see it. There is no advertising on VINALL and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

What is stored

  • Your account — the email address you sign up with, and an encrypted password. Handled by Supabase Auth; we never see your password.
  • Your music — the albums and songs you rate, your scores, notes, lists and diary entries.
  • Your lore — the answers you tap on Today, plus any optional note you add.
  • Your profile — username, avatar, bio, and anything else you choose to fill in.
  • Usage events — which questions were shown, answered or skipped, so we can tell whether the app is any good. These are tied to your account but contain no message content.
  • Crash reports — if something breaks, the error message and page address are recorded so it can be fixed.

What other people can see

VINALL is partly social, so some of this is public by design:

  • Public: your username, avatar, bio, ratings, lists, and lore answers you have not marked private.
  • Private: your email address, your usage events, and anything you mark private.

Your ratings visibility can be changed on your profile.

Other services

  • Supabase stores the database and handles logins (EU region).
  • Vercel hosts the site.
  • Spotify provides album artwork and catalogue information. Browsing VINALL does not tell Spotify who you are.
  • kworb.net provides public streaming totals used by the Bid Wars game. No personal data is sent there.

Deleting everything

Go to your profile and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your ratings, lore, lists, grooves, profile and login. It cannot be undone, and there is no waiting period.

Age

You must be 13 or over to use VINALL. If you are under 18, please do not post anything that identifies where you live or go to school. If you believe someone under 13 has an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be erased. Deleting your account does the last one immediately; for the others, get in touch.
